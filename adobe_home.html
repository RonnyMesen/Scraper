Is a 4×4 necessary in Costa Rica?

A 4×4 is necessary in Costa Rica if you are traveling to rural or mountainous areas where road conditions are unpaved or rugged. A 4WD is unnecessary for San Jose, Liberia, or the larger beach towns.

How are gas prices in Costa Rica?

As of 2026, fuel prices in Costa Rica are regulated by ARESEP and are generally around ₡753–₡756 per liter for gasoline, while diesel is lower at approximately ₡670 per liter. This is equivalent to roughly US$5.45–US$6.20 per gallon, depending on the fuel type and the current exchange rate. When planning your trip, we recommend estimating your fuel budget based on the type of vehicle, your driving distance, and the destinations you plan to visit. This is especially important if you will be traveling to remote beaches, national parks, or mountain areas, where gas stations may be less frequent. For planning, remember that gas prices in Costa Rica can change monthly.

  • Documents you need to rent a car in Costa Rica

    Documents you need to rent a car in Costa Rica include a driver’s license that has been valid for at least two years and a valid passport or original Costa Rican cedula. The passport must have a valid Costa Rica 90-day entry permit. An international driver’s license is required if your license is digital or written in a non-Latin alphabet. The minimum age for drivers is 23 years old. Debit cards are accepted for vehicle rentals, but the deposit must be made with a major credit card.

  • Best time to visit Costa Rica

    It’s always the best time to visit Costa Rica. Even during the rainy season, the mornings are generally sunny and clear.   • 4WD Car rental

    If your trip will mainly be on main roads and to easily accessible tourist destinations, you will usually not need a 4WD vehicle. However, if you plan to visit areas such as Monteverde, the Osa Peninsula, Santa Teresa, Nosara, Ostional, mountainous areas, or if you are traveling during the rainy season, we recommend renting a high-clearance SUV and preferably one with 4WD. For these types of routes, Adobe Rent a Car offers recommended options such as the Suzuki Vitara 4WD, Hyundai Tucson 4WD, Kia Sportage 4WD, Hyundai Santa Fe 4WD, or Mitsubishi Montero Sport 4WD, depending on availability. It is also important to check the exact location of your hotel, villa, or Airbnb, as in Costa Rica the destination itself may be accessible, but the final few kilometers may include gravel roads, steep hills, or more challenging access routes. Popular destinations such as Arenal and Rincón de la Vieja are also easily reached with a 4WD rental car.

  • Driving Tips for Costa Rica

    Our Adobe driving tips for Costa Rica: obey all traffic rules, take it slow, and drive during the day whenever possible. Driving laws in Costa Rica are similar to the United States, Canada, and Western Europe. However, some roads in Costa Rica are incredibly steep, rough, and in poor condition. Heavy rains, fog, washouts, and potholes are common, especially during the rainiest months. Watch out for construction and roads that narrow to one lane before a bridge.
